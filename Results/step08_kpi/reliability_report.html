5. Methodology

  • SMC: Sequential Monte Carlo, two-state Markov chain (TTF ~ Exponential, TTR ~ LogNormal)
  • FOR computation: Unplanned outages only (K4=trip, K7=fault, K22=boiler tube failure)
  • Hazard factors: Accident-induced failure rate amplification (typhoon events)
  • Convergence: CoV(EENS) < 2% with minimum 50 simulation years
  • Bootstrap CI: Non-parametric, 2000 iterations, 95% confidence
  • OPF: DC optimal power flow with VOLL = 6,000,000 NT$/MWh for load shedding
